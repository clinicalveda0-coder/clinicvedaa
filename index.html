🌿 Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

Ayurvedic View: Balya aur Rasayana dravya, jo immunity aur strength ko badhata hai.

Modern Science: Adaptogen jo body me cortisol (stress hormone) ko kam karta hai.

🍃 Triphala

Ayurvedic View: Tridosha hara formulation jo digestion aur bowel habit ko regulate karta hai.

Modern Science: High antioxidants aur prebiotic capabilities gut microbiome ko health rakhti hain.

🌱 Brahmi (Bacopa monnieri)

Ayurvedic View: Medhya Rasayana jo memory aur mental clarity badhata hai.

Modern Science: Bacosides brain cell connectivity aur focus ko enhance karte hain.
